# WRATHOD (Petari's gamex version) clears the STE video registers $FF820C,
# $FF820E and $FF8264 on entering each section, whatever machine it runs on:
# the loader hands it A4 = 1 on an ST too. On a plain ST those addresses
# bus-error, its bus-error vector points at nothing, and the screen goes
# black at the first "disk change" - on this emulator (22 Nov 2026: the real
# ST board answered the write with BERR) and in Hatari 2.4.1 the same
# (--machine st, TOS 2.06: "Bus Error writing at address $ffff820c,
# PC=$660"), while on an STE it plays. The three writes become NOPs: on an
# ST they have nothing to clear. Checked in Hatari, plain ST: plays through
# the first section, loses a life and restarts it.
